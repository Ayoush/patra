
| Alias                                   | Canonical                                          | Why / When                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| --------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Orissa                                  | Odisha                                             | Renamed by the Orissa (Alteration of Name) Act and the Constitution (113th Amendment) Act, 2011, in force from November 2011. The old spelling was an anglicised form of the Odia name. The change also corrected the language name from "Oriya" to "Odia".                                                              |
| Pondicherry                             | Puducherry                                         | Union Territory. Renamed by the Pondicherry (Alteration of Name) Act, 2006, effective 1 October 2006. It restores the local (Tamil) form of the name. It covers the enclaves of Puducherry, Karaikal, Mahe, and Yanam.                                                                                                   |
| Uttaranchal                             | Uttarakhand                                        | Created on 9 November 2000, carved out of Uttar Pradesh, under the name Uttaranchal. Renamed Uttarakhand effective 1 January 2007, after sustained local demand for the historical regional name.                                                                                                                        |
| Bombay, Bombay State                    | Maharashtra                                        | Bombay State was the bilingual (Marathi and Gujarati) state formed in 1950 from the Bombay Presidency. The Bombay Reorganisation Act, 1960 split it along linguistic lines into Maharashtra and Gujarat, effective 1 May 1960. This alias is about the *state*. The city's rename is covered under "City renames" below. |
| Madras, Madras State                    | Tamil Nadu                                         | Madras State was the successor to the Madras Presidency. It was renamed Tamil Nadu on 14 January 1969 under the Madras State (Alteration of Name) Act, 1968. The new name reflects the state's linguistic identity.                                                                                                      |
| Mysore, Mysore State                    | Karnataka                                          | Mysore State was formed in 1956 by merging Kannada-speaking regions. It was renamed Karnataka on 1 November 1973, in line with the broader Kannada-speaking region it now covered.                                                                                                                                       |
| Travancore-Cochin                       | Kerala                                             | Travancore and Cochin were merged in 1949. Kerala was formed on 1 November 1956 under the States Reorganisation Act, 1956, by combining Travancore-Cochin with the Malabar district and Kasaragod taluk, both of which had been in Madras State.                                                                         |
| United Provinces, UP (colonial)         | Uttar Pradesh                                      | The colonial United Provinces of Agra and Oudh were renamed Uttar Pradesh on 24 January 1950, with the Constitution coming into force.                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Central Provinces and Berar             | Madhya Pradesh                                     | Renamed Madhya Pradesh in 1950. In 1956 it was enlarged by merging Madhya Bharat, Vindhya Pradesh, and Bhopal. Chhattisgarh was split off in 2000.                                                                                                                                                                       |
| East Punjab                             | Punjab                                             | East Punjab was the Indian portion of Punjab after the 1947 Partition. It was renamed Punjab in 1950. PEPSU (Patiala and East Punjab States Union) was merged into it in 1956. Haryana was carved out in 1966.                                                                                                           |
| NEFA, North-East Frontier Agency        | Arunachal Pradesh                                  | NEFA was the administrative name for the area before 1972. It became the Union Territory of Arunachal Pradesh in 1972 and a full state on 20 February 1987.                                                                                                                                                              |
| Laccadive, Minicoy and Amindivi Islands | Lakshadweep                                        | Union Territory. The unwieldy colonial-era name was replaced by Lakshadweep ("one hundred thousand islands") on 1 November 1973.                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Delhi                                   | National Capital Territory of Delhi (NCT of Delhi) | The Constitution (69th Amendment) Act, 1991 gave Delhi its current special status, with a Legislative Assembly, from 1992. Both forms are common in sources, so both are matched.                                                                                                                                        |
| Daman and Diu, Dadra and Nagar Haveli   | Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu           | Two Union Territories merged into one on 26 January 2020. Older data still lists them separately.                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
